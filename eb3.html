Steps in the EB-3 Visa Process

Learn how the EB-3 visa helps you become a Permanent Resident of the United States.

1

Step 1: Job Offer

Apply for a position. Interview. Receive and accept your EB-3 Job Offer Letter.

2

Step 2: PERM Filing
(Labor Certification)

Employer files the PERM labor certification with the Department of Labor to demonstrate no local qualified U.S. workers are available for the job.

3

Step 3: I-140 Petition Filing

Employer files the I-140 petition with USCIS to confirm the foreign worker's eligibility and the employer's ability to pay the offered wage.

4

Step 4: Adjustment of Status or Consular Processing

You file for Adjustment of Status (Form I-485) if you are in the U.S., or proceed with consular processing if you are outside the U.S.

5

Step 5: Arrival in the U.S. as a Permanent Resident

You enter the U.S. as a permanent resident and begin work for your employer for at least one year.

Frequently Asked Questions

What type of jobs are available under EB-3 Unskilled?
Roles typically include positions in food processing, warehousing, janitorial services, and hospitality across various US states where finding willing domestic labor is heavily constrained.
What is the realistic timeline for the process?
Expect 2.5 to 3 years for complete documentation approvals. Total time from initiation to the Embassy interview is governed by the US Visa Bulletin and currently averages 4–5 years.
What happens to my family after we migrate?
Spouses receive E34/EW4 visas identical to permanent residency and can work for any employer in the US immediately upon arrival. Children receive full rights to schooling.
Do you offer refunds if the application fails?
Yes. We provide completely free refiling or structural refunds if a denial occurs due to a clerical error or oversight made by Edu Abroad Limited. We stand by our work.
