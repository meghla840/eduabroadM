About Edu Abroad

Since 2016, we've been the trusted bridge between ambitious Bangladeshi students and the world's leading universities — turning study-abroad dreams into approved visas, packed suitcases, and new beginnings.

Our Story

Built by people who've walked this path

Edu Abroad Limited was founded on a simple belief: every deserving student should have honest, expert guidance when applying abroad — without the confusion, the guesswork, or the shortcuts that put a visa at risk.

What started as a small counseling desk in Gulshan, Dhaka has grown into a full-service consultancy spanning university admissions, scholarships, visa filing, and post-arrival support across the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Europe, and New Zealand.

Today, our team of counselors, visa specialists, and partnership managers has helped thousands of students walk confidently into their first day on a new campus.
