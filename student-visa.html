Be Prepared

General Student
Visa Requirements

University Offer Letter

Conditional or unconditional admission letter from a recognized institution.

English Proficiency

IELTS, TOEFL, or PTE scores as required by the university and country.

Proof of Funds

Bank statements showing sufficient funds for tuition + living expenses.

Valid Passport

A machine-readable passport valid for at least 6 months beyond your intended study period.

Academic Transcripts

Certified copies of all academic records from SSC, HSC, or Bachelor's degree.

Statement of Purpose

A compelling essay explaining your academic goals and reasons for choosing the institution.
